The Problem

Students waste up to an hour finding parking — every day.

Campus parking congestion is a chronic, high-severity problem affecting thousands of commuter students and faculty each day. Current solutions don't work — and the data proves it.

"I didn't leave enough time to find parking. I tried the paid lot closest to my class — it was full. Checking probably cost me 7 minutes, and I ended up late to a friend's presentation."

— Anna Knorr, Co-Founder, Fall 2025

Inaccurate existing data
ParkMobile's availability feature shows open spots that are actually full — data that relied on paying customers. Students learned not to trust it, making the feature worse than useless.
Real consequences
Students arrive late to class, park in the wrong zone and receive tickets, and drive aggressively through lots searching for spaces — creating genuine safety risks on campus.
No guidance before you go
Students have no way to know which lot to target before leaving their building. So they guess — and waste time circling when they guess wrong.

5 Whys — Root Cause Analysis

1
Why do students have trouble finding parking?
Because the lot is usually already filled up by the time they arrive.
2
Why is the lot already full?
Because students don't get there early enough — rush hours cause unpredictable delays.
3
Why don't students plan to leave earlier?
Because they try other lots closer to their building first, which are also full — wasting more time.
4
Why do they try those lots first?
Because they don't know if the spot will be taken, and the time saved if it's open seems worth the risk.
5
Why don't they know the lot status?
Because there is no reliable, real-time map of parking spot availability. That's what SmartPark solves.
The Solution

Know where to park before you leave.

SmartPark combines low-cost computer vision cameras, cloud processing, and a mobile app to give students real-time lot availability and pre-departure guidance — all within your university's existing infrastructure.

01
Camera Detects
Low-cost cameras ($50–150) mounted on existing light posts detect spot obstructions using CV — no face or plate data captured.
02
Cloud Processes
Obstruction data is processed via university cloud infrastructure, combining live CV data with known historical fill patterns.
03
App Recommends
SmartPark recommends the best lot for your zone, class schedule, and time of day — before you even leave your building.
04
You Park
GPS navigation guides you to the specific lot and section via Apple Maps or Google Maps integration. On time, every time.

Technology Stack

CV Model
Obstruction Detection
Detects whether a spot is blocked — not what's blocking it. Far simpler and more reliable than full vehicle recognition. No biometric data at all.
Edge
Arduino Uno Q
On-device edge computing for low-latency inference, reducing dependence on cloud connectivity. Identified by Arrow Representative Jeffery Hill.
Cloud
University-Hosted Server
Pattern modeling and app data served from university infrastructure — keeping data within institutional control and meeting privacy requirements.
Auth
SSO + DUO 2FA + Permit Check
University single sign-on with DUO two-factor authentication ensures only valid permit holders see the lots they're authorized to use.
Mobile
iOS & Android App
Native mobile app with Apple Maps and Google Maps integration for turn-by-turn navigation to specific lots and zones.
AI
Predictive Pattern Model
Uses your campus's confirmed lot-fill patterns (lots fill in a known order, peak at 10:45 AM) to predict availability even when live camera data is unavailable.

Graceful Degradation

If camera data is unavailable due to weather, power, or connectivity issues, SmartPark automatically falls back to historical pattern-based predictions using the lot-fill sequences confirmed by your university's Parking Operations. Students still get guidance — just prediction-based rather than live. This makes SmartPark more resilient than any system that simply fails when hardware does.

The Key Insight

ParkMobile's availability feature relies on paying users to infer occupancy. If someone parks without paying, the spot appears open — producing false data. SmartPark's cameras detect actual obstructions independently of user behavior. No participation required. No false readings.

🏛
University data relationships
Once embedded in a university's IT infrastructure, switching costs are high. Historical lot-fill data becomes part of the prediction model — data a competitor would need to start over to replicate.
🧠
Campus-trained CV models
Each deployment trains the obstruction model on that campus's specific lots, lighting, and layout. Over time, SmartPark builds a library of pre-trained campus configs that make new deployments faster and cheaper.
📱
Daily student habit
Students who import their class schedule and receive daily parking suggestions build a daily-use habit. High engagement and deep schedule integration makes the app sticky — students don't switch mid-semester.
🔒
Privacy by design
SmartPark never looks for faces or license plates. Our cameras only detect whether a parking spot is open or occupied — no identity data is ever captured, stored, or processed.
